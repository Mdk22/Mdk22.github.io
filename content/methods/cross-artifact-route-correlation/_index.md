purpose: "Compare route families exposed by separate application-owned artefacts so shared public semantics can distinguish a high-signal internal candidate from a guess."

proves: "That the candidate follows the application's observed route model and is derived from supplied metadata rather than broad enumeration."
does_not_prove: "It does not prove that the route is live or vulnerable until a bounded runtime request returns a meaningful semantic result."
